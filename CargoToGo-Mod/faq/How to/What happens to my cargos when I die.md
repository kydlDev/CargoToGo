What happens to my cargos when I die?

They move to your gravestone together with the rest of your items and stay there, also after a relog. Use Take all (or move a cargo over by hand) to put them back into your inventory. While a cargo is in the gravestone it is locked: it cannot be dropped, moved to another container, unpacked or built from. If the world's death rules delete unequipped items, cargos are deleted like every other item.
