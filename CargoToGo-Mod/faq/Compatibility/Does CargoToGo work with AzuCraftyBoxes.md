Does CargoToGo work with AzuCraftyBoxes?

Yes, and it is optional. With [AzuCraftyBoxes](https://valheim.hexium.gg/mods/Azumatt/AzuCraftyBoxes) installed, packing also takes materials from the containers it reaches, following its own range, chest toggles, include / exclude rules, "leave one", drawers and bags. This works even with CargoToGo's own `[Containers] Range` set to 0. `[Containers] UseAzuCraftyBoxes` switches it off.
